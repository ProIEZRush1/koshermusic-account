Delete your Kosher Music account

App: Kosher Music - Jewish Songs (Android: com.koshermusic.app · iOS: Kosher Music) · Developer: Overcloud México

Option 1 – In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Kosher Music and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

If the app asks you to sign in again (a security check), sign in and tap Delete account again.

Option 2 – By email

Write to admin@overcloud.us from the email address of your account, with the subject “Delete my Kosher Music account”. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in identifier (Apple, Google or email login).
  • Your email address and profile name.
  • Your cloud library: playlists, favorites and saved songs.
  • The usage records linked to your account.

What is kept

Only anonymous, aggregated statistics (for example total plays per song) that cannot identify you. Songs downloaded for offline listening stay on your device until you uninstall the app.

Delete some data without deleting your account

You can remove playlists, favorites and downloads yourself in the app at any time, or ask us by email to delete specific data.
